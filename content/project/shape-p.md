**STRIDE-P** (Stoke-on-Trent Inclusive Delivery of Exercise for Parkinson's) and **SHAPE-P** (Staffordshire Health and Physical Activity Engagement for Parkinson's) test whether a structured, phased exercise programme is feasible, safe and acceptable for people living with Parkinson's. The findings will inform larger trials and future service delivery.

### Who it's for

Adults aged 30 to 75 with mild to moderate Parkinson's (Hoehn & Yahr stages 1 to 3) who are not currently meeting recommended physical activity levels.

### The programme

- **Weeks 1–4, Learn:** weekly face-to-face group sessions with an exercise specialist at the University of Staffordshire
- **Weeks 5–10, Transition:** weekly live online group sessions with the same coach and group
- **Weeks 11–18, Own it:** independent home exercise guided by exercise recordings
- Three behaviour change consultations to support goal setting and motivation

Each session combines aerobic, resistance and balance training. Physical function and quality of life are assessed at baseline, at 18 weeks, and at a 3-month follow-up.

### Final findings

- 19 people enrolled across three cohorts, and 78% of sessions were attended
- Remote sessions were as well attended as face-to-face ones (77% vs 79%)
- Sit-to-stand performance improved by 6 repetitions, and Timed Up and Go was 0.8 seconds faster
- Participants kept exercising after the programme, and 10 of 15 completers formed a peer support group

The findings were presented at the [CHAD 10 Year Celebration Conference](/talk/shape-p-final-findings/) in November 2026. Next, the phased model is being extended to multiple sclerosis (SHAPE-MS), breast cancer (SHAPE-BCa) and other long-term conditions.
