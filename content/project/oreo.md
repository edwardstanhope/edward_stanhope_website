OREO compares the outcomes that exercise oncology randomised controlled trials registered in advance with the outcomes they went on to publish, to measure outcome omission and switching in the field.

Edward joined the team after the original preprint, to contribute to the updated review.

**Original preprint:** [medRxiv, 2021](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.03.12.21253378v1)
