PROVERB (Prehabilitation and RecOVERy from surgery for Breast cancer) tested the feasibility of a remotely supervised, multi-phasic, multi-modal exercise programme for women before and after breast cancer surgery. The programme combined aerobic, resistance and targeted exercise with behaviour change support based on the Health Action Process Approach, dyadic coping and wearable technology.

Remote exercise before and after surgery proved feasible and safe: five of six feasibility targets were met, including 76% study completion and 88% combined adherence. These findings now need confirming in an adequately powered multicentre trial.

- **Registration:** [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05901142](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05901142)
- **Presented at:** ISEO 2026 (Heidelberg), Beyond Futures Conference 2026 (University of Wolverhampton) and the HEPS Summer Mini Research Conference 2025 (University of Staffordshire)
