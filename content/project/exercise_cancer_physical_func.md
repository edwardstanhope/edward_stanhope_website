This systematic review and meta-analysis examines how exercise interventions affect physical function in patients with breast cancer, and forms part of Edward's PhD.

Of 5,381 records screened, 98 reports of 76 randomised controlled trials were included. Of these, 51 trials contributed to at least one meta-analysis and 25 were summarised narratively.

- **Protocol:** [PROSPERO CRD42022289197](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/display_record.php?ID=CRD42022289197)
