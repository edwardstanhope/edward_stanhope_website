abstract = "A systematic review and meta-analysis of 49 studies looking at whether low skeletal muscle mass in people with upper gastrointestinal cancer having systemic treatment is linked to severe (grade 3–4) adverse events and to stopping treatment early. Low muscle mass was associated with a higher risk of grade 3–4 adverse events (RR 1.44, 95% CI 1.23–1.68) and of treatment discontinuation (RR 2.39, 95% CI 1.87–3.07). Most studies had a high risk of bias, so prospective confirmatory studies are needed."
