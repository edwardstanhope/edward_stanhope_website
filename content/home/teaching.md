**Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA)**, awarded September 2019, recognising sustained leadership and impact in teaching and learning.

### Programme leadership

- **March 2026:** led the 10:30 credit restructure and revalidation of the BSc and FdSc programmes, University of Staffordshire
- **February 2025:** led the BSc Sports Therapy accreditation change from the Society of Sports Therapy (SST) to the British Association of Sport Rehabilitators and Trainers (BASRaT), University of Staffordshire
- **June 2024:** led the revalidation of the MSc Clinical Trials, London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine
- **March 2022:** led the successful business case securing a dedicated rehabilitation zone and greenscreen studio for simulation and telehealth learning, University of Staffordshire
- **February 2022:** developed and validated the new FdSc Rehabilitation and Manual Therapies, widening access and addressing sector needs, University of Staffordshire
- **January 2022:** led the 20:40 credit restructure and revalidation of the BSc and FdSc programmes, University of Staffordshire

As Programme Director of the MSc Clinical Trials at LSHTM, Edward has also led the programme's digital teaching and learning strategy, its recruitment, conversion and retention strategy, and a bespoke commercial training offer that secured a partnership with an overseas organisation.

### Research community

- **Slice of Research**, University of Staffordshire (founder, 2019; no longer running): a monthly cross-disciplinary forum that brought researchers, lecturers and students together in a relaxed, inclusive setting to share and discuss research. It welcomed contributors from all research backgrounds, gave researchers and postgraduate students a platform to showcase their work, and helped keep students and staff connected during the COVID-19 pandemic.
