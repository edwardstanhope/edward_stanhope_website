Welcome to my blog. I'm a Senior Lecturer in Sport Therapy at Staffordshire University and Co-Programme Director of the MSc Clinical Trials (Distance Learning) at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.

I'll use this space to write about:

- **Exercise and non-communicable disease**, including my PhD work on physical activity for women with breast cancer
- **Clinical trial methodology**, from recruitment and retention to design choices that make trials more useful
- **Teaching research methods** to undergraduate and postgraduate students
- **Practical statistics in R**, with short worked examples

## Writing a new post

In RStudio, use *Addins → New Post* (blogdown). Choose `.Rmarkdown` or `.Rmd` if the post includes R code, or `.md` for plain text. Posts live in `content/post/`.
